# Sources

Reference material behind the flashcard/quiz content, beyond the user's own course notes. Chosen for being canonical/widely-adopted, not just the first search result — see rationale per course. Content itself is written fresh (not copied) from these; only facts and structure are drawn from them.

## Research Techniques & Publication Ethics
- Steneck, *On Being a Scientist: A Guide to Responsible Conduct in Research* (4th ed., National Academies Press) — free online; the standard US graduate RCR text, 200,000+ copies distributed since 1989.
- ICMJE, *Recommendations for the Conduct, Reporting, Editing, and Publication of Scholarly Work* (icmje.org) — the authorship-criteria standard (the four criteria referenced directly in the course content).
- COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics) guidelines and flowcharts (publicationethics.org) — the standard for misconduct, authorship-dispute, and duplicate-publication handling used by most journals.
- Macrina, *Scientific Integrity* (ASM Press) — case-study-based; used for realistic misconduct/QRP scenarios.

## Soil Fertility Analysis
- Havlin, Tisdale, Nelson & Beaton, *Soil Fertility and Fertilizers: An Introduction to Nutrient Management* (8th ed., Pearson) — the standard nutrient-management textbook.
- Brady & Weil, *The Nature and Properties of Soils* (15th ed., Pearson) — the most widely cited general soil science textbook worldwide.

## Soil Organic Matter Management
- Magdoff & Weil (eds.), *Soil Organic Matter in Sustainable Agriculture* (CRC Press) — research-level reference on SOM fractions, mineralization/immobilization, aggregate stability.
- *Building Soils for Better Crops* (4th ed., SARE) — free PDF, practice-oriented.

## Soil Biotechnology
- Sylvia, Fuhrmann, Hartel & Zuberer, *Principles and Applications of Soil Microbiology* — the standard senior-undergrad/entry-grad textbook.
- van Elsas, Trevors, Soares Rosado & Nannipieri (eds.), *Modern Soil Microbiology* (3rd ed., CRC Press) — more advanced/research-level; used for methods content (metagenomics, 16S rRNA).
